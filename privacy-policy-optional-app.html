Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2025

Optional Labs ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by Optional Labs.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked to you ("personal information"), including:

  • Contact information (such as name, email address)
  • Usage information (such as how you use our app and services)
  • Device information (such as IP address, browser type, operating system)

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect for various purposes, including to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Send you technical notices, updates, and support messages
  • Respond to your comments and questions
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve user experience

Referral Requests

We provide a feature that allows users to send job referral requests to employees. Please note:

  • All referral requests are the sole responsibility of the user.
  • Users must ensure that referral requests are sent with respect and professionalism.
  • Abusive, spammy, irritating, or illegal behavior associated with referral requests is strictly prohibited.
  • Optional Labs reserves the right to block or permanently remove a user account with or without prior notice if such behavior is detected.
  • We encourage all users to treat employees with courtesy when requesting referrals.

Sharing of Information

We may share your personal information with:

  • Service providers who perform services on our behalf
  • Professional advisors, such as lawyers and accountants
  • Government entities when required by law

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, such as:

  • Right to access and receive a copy of your personal information
  • Right to correct inaccurate information
  • Right to delete your information
  • Right to object to certain processing activities
