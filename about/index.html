Small apps that keep your data with you.

Optional Labs LLP is a small company in India. We make apps for everyday life: sign-in codes, credit cards, medical papers, government letters, travel stamps, job search, remote access to your Mac, and sleep and recovery.

Why we build this way

The things these apps handle are personal. We think they belong with you, not on our servers.

  • On your device first

    Each app reads, stores and works on your own device. What you put in stays there unless you choose to export it, share it or turn on an extra feature.

  • One job each

    Every app does one everyday job well, so it is easy to understand and easy to trust.

  • Show our work

    Where an app reads a paper or a letter, it shows the original next to what it read, so you can check it.

  • Plain words

    Short sentences, real numbers and no fine print you have to decode.
