Everyday apps that keep your data on your phone.

Optional Labs is a small product company in Bengaluru, India. We make apps for the paperwork of everyday life: sign-in codes, credit cards, medical papers and government letters.

Why we build this way

The things these apps handle are personal. We think they belong on your phone, not on our servers.

  • On the phone first

    Each app reads, stores and works on your phone. What you put in stays there unless you choose to export or back it up.

  • One job each

    Every app does one everyday job well, so it is easy to understand and easy to trust.

  • Show our work

    Where an app reads a paper or a letter, it shows the original next to what it read, so you can check it.

  • Plain words

    Short sentences, real numbers and no fine print you have to decode.
