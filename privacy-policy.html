This website

  • No cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • No forms, accounts or sign-in.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, to run and protect its service. That is covered by GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or use those records.

When you email us

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and keep them only as long as needed for that.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact
