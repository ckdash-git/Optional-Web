Explore the apps How we build

Four apps. One rule.

Each app does one everyday job, and keeps what you give it on your phone.

  1. ZeroAuth

    Authenticator for iPhone and Android

    A privacy-first authenticator. Your two-factor codes are made on the phone, with no internet needed, and kept in an encrypted vault.

    • Codes work offline
    • AES-256-GCM vault
    • Face ID and fingerprint lock
    Visit zeroauth.in App Store Google Play

  2. Saifu

    Credit card advisor for India

    Which card at checkout, and what you are about to lose. Saifu ranks your cards for each spend and shows fee waivers, monthly caps and points about to expire.

    • 336 Indian cards listed
    • Spends stay on your phone
    • No card numbers or bank login
    Visit saifu.in Find my best card

  3. Parchi

    Family medical papers

    Photograph a prescription, a lab report or a hospital bill. Parchi reads it on your phone, files it under the right family member and makes a one-page summary for the next doctor visit.

    • Read on the phone
    • Medicine reminders
    • Health card per person
    Visit getparchi.com Coming soon to App Store and Google Play

  4. Tegami

    City-hall letters, for residents of Japan

    Every letter from city hall, explained in plain English. Photograph a notice from the ward office, pension service, tax office or immigration, and Tegami tells you what it is, what to do and by when.

    • Deadlines pulled out
    • Era dates converted
    • Letters encrypted on the phone
    Visit Tegami Coming soon to the App Store
