Parchi

Family medical papers

Your family's medical papers, read and kept on your phone.

Photograph a prescription, a lab report or a hospital bill. Parchi reads it on your phone, files it under the family member it belongs to, and gives you a one-page summary for the next doctor visit.

Visit getparchi.com

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

The problem
Prescriptions, lab reports and hospital bills pile up in drawers and photo galleries, and the one you need is never at hand at the doctor's.
What we fixed
Parchi reads each paper on your phone, files it under the right family member and makes a one-page summary for the next visit.

Screens from the app

Swipe through the main screens, from the iPhone app with sample data.

How it works

  1. 1

    Photograph

    Take a picture of a prescription, a report or a bill, or choose one from your photos.

  2. 2

    Check

    Parchi reads the printed text on your phone and fills in dates, clinic and medicines. Correct anything before saving.

  3. 3

    Keep

    The paper is filed under the right person, easy to search, with medicine reminders and a summary for the doctor.

What you get

  • Reads printed papers

    Dates, clinics, amounts, medicines and test values, pulled out for you to check.

  • Import lab PDFs

    Share a report PDF to Parchi from Files, Mail, Gmail or WhatsApp.

  • Medicines and refills

    Tick each dose and get a reminder before a supply may run out.

  • Home readings

    Blood pressure, sugar and weight for each person, as a simple chart.

  • Every policy in one place

    Policy numbers, nominees, renewal reminders and the policy papers.

  • Insurance claim pack

    One PDF of the papers for a hospital stay, with a checklist and the date to send it by.

  • A summary for the doctor

    One page of what matters, with the original scans attached if you want.

  • English and Japanese

    Every screen, reminder and health card in either language.

Nothing leaves your phone

How it works

  • Papers are read on the phone: Apple Vision on iPhone, Google ML Kit on Android
  • Face ID, fingerprint or passcode lock
  • Backup to a file protected by your own password

What it never has

  • An account to make
  • A server to sign in to
  • Analytics

Parchi never says whether a reading is good or bad, and the camera pulse check is an estimate, not a medical device. Read Parchi's privacy policy.
