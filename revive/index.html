Revive

Sleep, recovery and HRV from your wearable

Answers, not dashboards.

Know how recovered you are, how you slept, and what today can take. From the wearable you already own, kept on your phone.

Visit revive.optionallabs.com

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Ask for an invite on the Revive site.

The problem
Most health apps hand you a wall of figures, and compare you with a stranger instead of with your own normal.
What we fixed
Revive says how you slept and how ready you are in plain words, then shows the number behind it, always against your own normal.

Inside Revive

From the app, with sample data. One score, with the reasons behind it.

How it works

  1. 1

    Connect your wearable

    The Amazfit Helio Strap works over Bluetooth. On iPhone, sleep and heart rate also come from Apple Health, and on Android from Health Connect.

  2. 2

    Read the answer

    Recovery, sleep and HRV in plain words first, then the number, compared with your own normal.

  3. 3

    Decide your day

    Train when your body says yes, with a bedtime for tonight worked out from what you owe.

What you get

  • Recovery with reasons

    One score, and the page behind it shows what moved it.

  • Sleep, drawn as a dive

    Awake is the surface; the line sinks through REM and light sleep to deep and comes back up.

  • HRV against your normal

    Compared with your own range, never a stranger's.

  • Workouts

    Your strap's heart rate drives live zones, a route map and a share picture made from your own photo.

  • Activity

    Steps, distance and calories, by hour and by day.

  • A bedtime for tonight

    Worked out from the sleep you owe.

  • Works with what you wear

    Amazfit Helio Strap, Apple Watch through Apple Health, and Galaxy, Pixel and Wear OS watches through Health Connect.

  • Export or delete any time

    Your data lives on your phone and you can export or delete it whenever you like.

Your health data stays on your phone

On your phone

  • No accounts, no analytics, advertising or tracking
  • Your data lives on your phone, and you can export or delete it

Only if you turn it on

  • AI explanations are off by default. When you tap Why? under a score, a short summary of that day's numbers, with a random id and no name or free text, goes to our server and on to Anthropic's Claude, which writes a sentence or two back. We keep none of it.

Revive is not medical advice. Its scores are estimates for personal insight; if a reading worries you, talk to a health professional. Read Revive's privacy policy.
