Saifu

Credit card advisor for India

Which card at checkout. And what you are about to lose.

Saifu tells you which of your cards pays most for this purchase. It also shows the fee waivers, monthly caps and points about to run out, and how many free lounge visits you have left.

Visit saifu.in Find my best card

Web tools live now. App coming soon.

The problem
With a few credit cards, it is hard to know which one pays most at checkout, and fee waivers, monthly caps and expiring points slip by.
What we fixed
Saifu ranks your cards for each spend and warns you before a fee waiver, a cap or your points run out.

Screens from the app

Swipe through the main screens. They use the app's sample wallet.

How it works

  1. 1

    Add your cards

    Pick the cards you hold from 336 cards issued in India. No card numbers and no bank login.

  2. 2

    Say where you are paying

    Type the shop and the amount, like Swiggy for 800 rupees.

  3. 3

    Use the card it picks

    Saifu ranks your cards with their caps and exclusions, and shows what each one gives back.

What you get

  • Which card?

    Ranks your cards, or a family member's, for a shop and an amount.

  • Fee waivers

    Progress through each card year and how much to spend each month to get the fee back.

  • Family wallet

    Cards tagged by person. Rank across everyone or one person.

  • Reward points

    Balance, rupee value and expiry, with reminders 30 and 7 days before.

  • Statement import

    Read a PDF statement on the phone. Its password is asked once and never stored.

  • Spends

    Monthly totals, categories and rewards earned, for 3, 6 or 12 months.

  • Foreign currency

    The real cost on each card after markup and GST, and what it earns.

  • Lounges and EMI

    Free lounge visits left, an EMI calculator with GST, and an eligibility check.

Your money stays private

On your phone

  • Your cards and spends
  • Statements, read on the phone
  • An encrypted backup file you control (AES-256-GCM)
  • Optional Face ID or fingerprint lock

Never asked for

  • An account or sign-up
  • Card numbers
  • Your bank login
  • Statement passwords, which are never stored

Some cards link to an affiliate offer. Those links are labelled, and they are looked up only after your cards are ranked, so they never change the answer.
