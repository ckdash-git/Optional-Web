How we build

  • Small and focused

    Each app does one everyday job, so it can be understood, finished and trusted.

  • Honest about data

    What an app sends off your device is written down, and the websites claim only what the apps do.

  • Built to be checked

    Where an app reads a paper or a letter, it shows the original beside what it read.

  • Plain words

    Short sentences and real numbers, on the screen and in the code comments.
