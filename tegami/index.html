Tegami

City-hall letters, for residents of Japan

Every letter from city hall, explained in plain English.

Photograph a notice from the ward office, the pension service, the tax office or immigration. Tegami tells you what it is, what you need to do, and by when, without the letter ever leaving your phone.

Visit Tegami

Coming soon to the App Store

The problem
Letters from Japanese government offices come in formal Japanese, with deadlines, amounts and era dates that are easy to miss.
What we fixed
Tegami tells you what the letter is, what to do and by when, in plain English, without the letter leaving your phone.

Letters Tegami explains

Common notices from Japanese offices, named and explained.

  • Resident tax
  • National Health Insurance
  • Pension
  • My Number
  • Immigration
  • Utilities

Screens will appear here when Tegami reaches the App Store.

How it works

  1. 1

    Photograph

    Take a picture of the notice, on your iPhone.

  2. 2

    Read the explanation

    What it is, what you need to do, and by when, with deadlines and amounts pulled out.

  3. 3

    Check it yourself

    See the original and the exact text that was read, next to the explanation.

What you get

  • What it is

    Resident tax, National Health Insurance, pension, My Number, immigration and utilities, named and explained.

  • What to do, and by when

    Deadlines and amounts pulled out, with era dates like 令和8年 converted for you.

  • Check it yourself

    The original letter and the exact text that was read, beside the explanation.

  • Shared with your family

    Three letters free. Then ¥1,800 a year or ¥4,500 once, shared with your family.

Nothing leaves your phone

On your iPhone

  • Your letters and their photos, encrypted on the device
  • Moved to a new iPhone only by your own encrypted backup

What it never has

  • An account
  • A server
  • Analytics

Tegami has no accounts and no servers, so there is nothing to reset or recover online.
